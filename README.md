# Solar energy Premium Template - Strategic Architecture

## 1. Sitemap & Content Architecture

### Public-Facing Website
- **Homepage**: High-impact hero, trust bar, service overview, "Why Solar" (benefits), simplified calculator preview, recent projects, testimonials, and SEO-optimized footer.
- **About Page**: Company story (template), mission/vision, team grid, certifications/awards, and partnership logos.
- **Services Page**: Comprehensive overview of Residential, Commercial, and Industrial offerings.
    - **Individual Service Pages**: Dedicated landing pages for:
        - Residential Solar (Roof/Ground)
        - Commercial & Industrial Solar
        - Solar Maintenance & Cleaning
        - Battery Storage & EV Charging
- **Projects Gallery**: Filterable grid (Residential, Commercial, Institutional).
    - **Project Details Page**: Case study layout with specs, savings data, and gallery.
- **Testimonials Page**: Video and text testimonials with "Verified Customer" badges.
- **Solar Calculator**: Interactive tool for estimating savings and system size.
- **Blog**: Educational content on ROI, technology, and policy.
- **Contact Page**: Multi-step lead form, map, and WhatsApp floating CTA.

### Admin Dashboard (Template Internal)
- **Leads Manager**: CRM-style board for tracking quote requests.
- **Project Manager**: CMS for uploading new installation case studies.
- **Content CMS**: Management for testimonials, team members, services, and blog posts.
- **Global Settings**: Branding toggle (logos, colors, contact info).

## 2. User Journey & Conversion Strategy

### The "Sunny Path" to Conversion
1.  **Awareness**: High-quality hero image showing a premium installation with a clear "Calculate Your Savings" CTA.
2.  **Trust**: Immediate visibility of "Trust Bar" (Partners, Certifications, and Total Megawatts installed).
3.  **Validation**: Browse Project Gallery or read specific Case Studies to see localized proof.
4.  **Action**: 
    - **Primary CTA**: Multi-step Quote Request (Lead Gen).
    - **Secondary CTA**: "Chat on WhatsApp" (Low-friction inquiry).
5.  **Retention**: Educational blog content and newsletter signup for long-term real estate developer leads.

### CRO Features
- **Sticky Header**: "Get a Quote" always visible.
- **Social Proof**: Real-time "Recent Installation" toast notifications.
- **Value-First**: The Solar Calculator provides immediate value before asking for contact info.

## 3. Component Inventory

### Global Components
- **Navigation**: Responsive Mega-menu with Service icons.
- **Footer**: SEO-friendly links, social icons, newsletter signup.
- **Floating CTA**: WhatsApp toggle for instant messaging.

### UI Components
- **Hero Sections**: Split layout (Visual + Form) or Centered Impact.
- **Stats Cards**: Big bold numbers for "Years in Business," "CO2 Saved," "Total Savings."
- **Service Cards**: Hover-animated cards with clear "Learn More" actions.
- **Project Cards**: Image-heavy cards with overlay meta-data.
- **Testimonial Slider**: Premium card design with star ratings and photos.
- **Multi-step Form**: High-converting lead capture form.
- **Solar Calculator Widget**: Interactive slider and input UI.

## 4. Design System (Preview)
- **Palette**: #F9B233 (Yellow), #0B2D5B (Blue), #071A35 (Navy), #FFFFFF (White), #27AE60 (Green).
- **Typography**: Inter (Sans-serif) for high readability and tech-forward feel.
- **Border Radius**: 12px - 16px for a modern, soft-tech look.
- **Shadows**: Deep, soft elevations to create a layered "Dashboard" feel on the web.
