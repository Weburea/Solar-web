Frequently Asked Questions

Commercial solar systems typically see a return on investment within 4 to 7 years, depending on local utility rates, available incentives, and the system's size. After the payback period, the energy generated is essentially free for the remaining 20+ years of the system's lifespan.

Solar panels require very little maintenance as they have no moving parts. Occasional cleaning (1-2 times a year) to remove dust or debris is usually sufficient. Our smart monitoring systems will automatically alert you if performance drops, indicating maintenance might be needed.

While solar panels are most efficient in direct sunlight, they still generate significant power on cloudy or overcast days (typically 10-25% of their normal capacity). Modern tier-1 panels are designed to capture a broader spectrum of light, making them highly effective even in less-than-ideal weather conditions.
